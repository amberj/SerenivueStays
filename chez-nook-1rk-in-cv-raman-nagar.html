Price

Minimum booking duration: 2 days (mandatory).

For Direct Bookings: INR (Indian Rupees) 2000 per night (for stays less than 7 days).

Planning to stay for long term? We offer discounts for longer stays (based on stay duration as well as amenities needed). Please connect with us on Whatsapp Chat so that we can discuss the same. 

Important points to keep in mind:

  • You can pay via UPI (if you are from India) or via Paypal (if you are based outside India). Cash payments are not accepted.
  • There is no security deposit, but 100% of amount needs to be paid before checkin.
  • Refunds and cancellations: No cancellation and no refunds on direct bookings via this website. If you want a refundable booking, please book via Airbnb as the Airbnb listing for this house has an option for refundable booking (with Airbnb taking charge of enforcing the cancellation policy as well as process refunds).

Availability

Due to proximity of this flat to key work center (Bagmane Tech Park, CV Raman Nagar), many of our guests end up extending their stay in this property depending on their work commitments. 

You can message us on Whatsapp with your check-in and checkout dates and we will share availability with you.
